Min web is the thinnest metal left anywhere, including at the edge; about the sheet thickness is a safe start. Min hole is the smallest finished hole worth cutting, and is raised to 1.5× the kerf if it is below that.

Behind the holes

Removed metal reads as white. The preview colors are only for looking at: the tone model is always "open area = brightness".

mm

Top sheet first, solid base last. Deeper holes are offset from the one above by the registration allowance on every side, so a small misalignment when stacking does not show a sliver of the wrong color. Where colors blend within a hole they nest smaller; where a hole shows one sheet they are cut larger and hide under the top sheet. The base shows only through every hole, so give it the color the image needs least.

Pattern

Output

One file per sheet, in mm. Holes are blue, the outline is red (in DXF, layers HOLES and OUTLINE): cut blue first, so the piece stays in the sheet until its holes are done. Paths are already offset for the kerf, so turn off kerf offset in the laser software. DXF is R12; if your software asks for its units, they are millimeters. DXF names end in the sheet's color.
